Franchise FAQs

What is the total investment required?

The total investment starts from ₹8 Lakhs for a Tier 3 city unit, going up to ₹15-20 Lakhs for a Tier 1 Metro flagship store. This includes equipment, interiors, and franchise fee.

What is the expected ROI?

Our franchisees typically see a Return on Investment (ROI) within 12-18 months. With a gross margin of 70-80%, operational break-even can be achieved in the first 3 months.

Do I need prior experience in laundry business?

No experience is mandated. We provide comprehensive 15-day training for you and your staff, covering operations, machine handling, chemical usage, and customer management.
